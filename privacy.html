Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

AI News Shorts Uploader ("the App") is used only by its operator to manage the operator's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

  • YouTube Data API: upload videos to the operator's channel and list that channel's videos and basic statistics (views, likes).
  • YouTube Analytics API: read aggregate analytics for the operator's own videos (e.g. average view percentage).

How data is used

Data is used only to publish videos and to analyse which of the operator's videos performed better, so that future videos can be improved. Aggregate statistics may be stored in the operator's private code repository.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties. Video titles and statistics may be sent to Google's Gemini API solely to generate the analysis described above. No data about other YouTube users is collected.

Retention and revocation

Stored statistics can be deleted at any time by the operator. Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions.

The App uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
